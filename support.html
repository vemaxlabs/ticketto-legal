How do I add a ticket?

Open the ticket PDF or screenshot in any app, tap Share, and choose Ticketto. You can also open Ticketto and import a file, scan with the camera, or paste ticket text. Check the details on the review screen, then add the pass to Apple Wallet.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Smart extraction uses Apple Intelligence where it is available and turned on. Without it, Ticketto still reads tickets, but you may need to correct more fields by hand.

A field was read wrongly. What do I do?

Every field can be edited before the pass is created, and again later from the pass screen. Date and time suggestions appear under the date picker when the ticket contains other likely dates.

Will the venue accept my pass?

Ticketto copies the barcode or QR code from your original ticket. We cannot guarantee that an operator or venue will accept it, so keep your original ticket as a backup.

Does Ticketto upload my tickets?

No. Reading your tickets, the smart features and the creation of the pass all happen on your device. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm. Apple then asks you to confirm with Sign in with Apple, which also removes Ticketto's access to your Apple ID. This permanently deletes your account and the records we hold about you, removes every pass and saved file in the app from your device (and from iCloud if you turned on sync), and returns the app to its first-launch state. It cannot be undone. Passes you already added to Apple Wallet stay in Wallet; you can remove them there.

If you cannot open the app, email us from the address linked to your Apple account and we will delete the account for you.

How do I turn Apple Intelligence on?

Go to Settings, then Apple Intelligence & Siri on your iPhone. Availability depends on your device and region.
