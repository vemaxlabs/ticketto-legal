Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026 · Ticketto by Vemax Labs

Ticketto is built to keep your tickets on your device. This page explains what we do store, why, and how you can delete it.

What stays on your device

Your ticket files (PDFs, photos, screenshots), the text and barcodes read from them, the passes you create, trips, reminders and settings are stored on your iPhone. The smart features (field suggestions, trip grouping, duplicate detection, card styling, the year recap and questions about a ticket) run on your device using Apple's on-device models. None of this is sent to us or to any third party.

What we store on our servers

  • Account record: an identifier from Sign in with Apple and, if you choose to share them, the name and email Apple provides.
  • Terms acceptance: which version of the terms you accepted and when.
  • Access status: whether your account has any purchase or promo code recorded. The app is currently free.
  • Diagnostic events: short usage and error messages (for example "sign in succeeded"), linked to your account identifier, used to fix problems. They do not contain ticket content.

This data is held with our backend provider, Supabase, in Singapore. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. There is no tracking and no third-party advertising or analytics in the app.

Apple services you may choose to use

Sign in with Apple is how you sign in; Apple handles your credentials. If you turn on iCloud Sync, your passes are copied between your own devices through your own iCloud account, and we cannot see them. Apple Wallet stores the passes you add to it.

Permissions

Camera and Photos are used only when you scan or choose a ticket. Calendar, Location, Notifications and Face ID or Touch ID are optional and used only for the reminder or lock feature you turn on.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete Account. This deletes your account and all the server records listed above, and removes every pass and saved file in the app from your device (and iCloud, if sync is on). Deleted data cannot be recovered. You can also email vemaxlabs@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Retention

We keep server records while your account exists and delete them when you delete your account.

Children

Ticketto is not directed at children and is intended for people aged 16 or over, or the age of digital consent in your country if higher.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, ask you to review them in the app.
