Ticketto

Tickets to Apple Wallet, on your iPhone.

Ticketto turns the PDFs, screenshots and photos of tickets you already have (trains, buses, movies, flights, events and stays) into Apple Wallet passes. It reads your ticket on your device, lets you check every detail, and builds the pass on your device. Your tickets are not uploaded.
